SQL JOINs

Let's see how different joins combine data from two tables.

06

INNER JOIN — Without ON

First, observe what happens when the join condition is not specified. 

# Without on
select name,department_name from employees as e
inner join department as d;
07

INNER JOIN — With ON

The ON condition specifies how the two tables are related. 

# With on
select name,department_name from employees as e
inner join department as d
on e.department_id = d.department_id;
08

Add a New Employee

This employee has department_id 4. 

INSERT INTO employees VALUES
(104, 'Rahul', 4);
09

LEFT JOIN

Observe what happens when all records from the left table are retained. 

#Left join
select e.name,d.department_name from employees as e
left join department as d
on e.department_id = d.department_id;
10

RIGHT JOIN

Observe what happens when all records from the right table are retained. 

# Right join
select e.name,d.department_name from employees as e
right join department as d
on e.department_id = d.department_id;
11

CROSS JOIN

Observe how every employee is combined with every department. 

# Cross join 
select e.name,d.department_name from employees as e
cross join department as d;
